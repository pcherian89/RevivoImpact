# Revivo Impact — fictional world and data

**This file is the source of truth for every name and number.** Every other document must match it. All people,
organisations, places, competitions, amounts and records are invented. All amounts are USD (§11). Every figure here
respects the invariants in `02` §6.2 and is re-computed from records by the seed (no total is typed in).

**Revised in P1 (25 Nov 2026):** a multi-organisation ecosystem (9 funders including one verified individual sponsor,
seeking organisations on both sides, open, upcoming and closed opportunities, 16 funding requests); renamed names after
the P1 name check (§7); every "P1" item fixed (§8–9). Updated after the P1 expert review: line-item requests with
"covered from other sources" and holds, Highland Cycling Club funded by an individual sponsor, enough organisations
and minors' profiles for every count, expected values at all three demo moments (§9.4).

## 1. Principles

- Coherent, international and diverse: many sports, genders, ages and backgrounds; para sport included.
- Never real organisations or real people; no real countries or cities in the prototype (§12).
- **A genuine multi-organisation ecosystem**, not one foundation's portal: Discover always shows several independent
  funders, opportunities and requests.
- Enough records that nothing looks empty; not thousands of records for show.
- Every aggregate is computed from records by a seeded generator (fixed seed). If an illustrative aggregate is ever
  needed, it is labelled "illustrative".

## 11. Currency policy

- **USD only in the demonstration dataset** (`07` D4). The model stores every amount with its currency and groups
  every total by currency (`02` §6.4), so other currencies can be added later as **separately authored datasets**
  (amounts written for each currency, totals re-summed). Never a symbol swap and never an exchange-rate conversion;
  never a combined total across currencies.

## 12. Fictional geography

Regions use plain invented descriptors: **Coastal Region, Northern Region, Central Region, Lakes Region, Southern
Region, Highland Region**. No real countries, cities or landmarks. Competitions are fictional: National U21 Judo
Championships, Coastal Region Judo Open, Continental U21 Invitational, Continental Para Athletics Series, Ostral
National Age-Group Championships. Names of people come from many cultures; no single country's conventions dominate.
