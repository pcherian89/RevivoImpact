# Revivo Impact — image asset plan (P1, 25 Nov 2026)

For the founder, who will generate the final images in ChatGPT. It replaces the initial 14-image list (`04` §10) and
follows the revised screen architecture (`03` §4). Style and grading rules: `04` §9. Until an image arrives, the
prototype shows a dark placeholder of the same shape with the filename and "Illustrative".

## 1. What changed from the initial plan, and why

- **19 images instead of 14**, because the product now shows a multi-organisation ecosystem (more funders and funding
  types, an individual sponsor, commercial sponsorship, facilities and coach education). Each image earns its place: it
  either tells the viewer what kind of funding a screen is about, or marks a cinematic moment. No image fills space.
- **Dropped:** the separate phone version of the athlete Home (the 16:9 image is shown whole on phones) and the
  standalone "para athlete on the Discover tab" placement (now the Para Athlete Equipment Grant header and Nadia's
  request).
- **Added:** seeker Discover (many sports), judo training camp, commercial sponsorship (adult sprinter), facilities,
  coach education, individual sponsor (rowing club).
- **Readability (founder: "avoid excessively dark imagery"):** every prompt asks for the main subject to be clearly lit
  and readable; darkness only at the frame edges. The family grade stays (`04` §9). After the P1 review, **five
  scenes are in bright daylight** (im-03, im-05, im-10, im-12, im-15) and im-16 is daytime indoors, so the set is not
  all golden hour.
- **Casting is stated in every prompt** (age, gender and skin tone), so diversity is not left to the generator's
  defaults; the supporter in im-01 is a woman in her fifties.
- **Diversity across the set:** 12 sports (athletics, para athletics, judo, football, swimming, rowing, boxing,
  badminton, table tennis, cycling, basketball, wheelchair racing); women and men in equal measure; teenagers (always
  from behind, in profile or small), young adults, adults and older adults; disabled athletes; grassroots,
  developing and national-level settings; indoor and outdoor; dawn, golden hour and evening light.

## 2. Master block (paste once into ChatGPT, then one prompt per message)

> You are making a consistent series of photographs for Revivo Impact, a trustworthy platform that connects athletes,
> clubs and sports programmes with funders and sponsors, and helps funders see how their support is used. Style:
> authentic documentary sports photography in the same series as the Revivo website and Revivo Pathway: golden hour or
> warm evening light, one warm low light source (or warm window light indoors), muted warm natural colour, fine grain,
> quietly hopeful and real. **The main subject is always clearly lit and easy to read at a glance: faces, hands and
> the sporting action keep their detail.** Shadows deepen to near-black (#0A0A0B) only towards the edges of the frame,
> never across faces. Settings anywhere in the world, no landmarks. People: mixed genders, ages, skin tones and body
> types; include disabled athletes where natural. Coaches wear plain dark sportswear with one subtle, thin lime-green
> trim line (not neon, not glowing); funders and staff wear plain smart-casual clothes; athletes wear plain unbranded sportswear in muted warm
> colours (navy, terracotta, ochre, white, grey). Anyone under 18 is seen from behind, in profile or small in the
> frame, never in close-up, and always with several people or a parent present in an open setting. Every person is
> entirely inside the frame (no cut-off heads, hands or feet). No text, letters, numbers, bibs, logos, flags or
> signage; papers and screens face away or are unreadable. No money, cheques, cash, coins, handshake poses, trophies or
> medals. No holograms, overlays, data graphics, glowing lines, neon, lens flares, blue or purple light. Make every
> image a wide landscape (or a tall portrait when I say so) and keep every person well inside the frame with generous
> margins on all sides, because each image will be trimmed once to 16:9 (or 9:16). Reply "Ready".

## 3. The images

Ratios: the final files are **16:9 (1672×941)** or **9:16 (941×1672)**. ChatGPT usually produces 1536×1024 (landscape)
or 1024×1536 (portrait); each image is trimmed **once, during preparation**, to 16:9 or 9:16, keeping every person whole
inside a margin of about 8%. On screen every image is then shown whole in its own frame, fading at its edges, never
cropped again.

## 5. Rejection checklist

Reject and regenerate any image with: lettering, logos, bibs, lane markings or numbers; a cropped figure; a minor's face in close-up
or a minor alone with an adult; money, a handshake or ceremony staging; a fake interface or light streaks; **the main
subject in shadow or faces lost in darkness**; a brighter, flatter look that stands apart from the Pathway set;
stereotyped or tokenistic casting; landmarks or recognisable places.

## 6. Pipeline

Tone each image with the same solver and target as Pathway (`tools/tone/solve.py`, copied with a source note in P4):
p75 luminance ≈ 76/255, mean saturation ≈ 42%, clamps as `04` §9; record values in `src/content/images.ts`; compress
to WebP (about 1672px wide, quality 78) for the single-file build; total images under about 3 MB. Every image is
labelled "Illustrative" in its alt text context and is never presented as a real participant.
