# Owner's strategy document (pasted, from a ChatGPT research session) — summary of its contents

1. Recommends "Programme Delivery & Impact" as the second prototype, repositioned as an OPERATIONAL product used all year (not a quarterly reporting tool): "Help sports organisations deliver funded programmes, identify problems as they occur, demonstrate where money is being spent, and provide credible evidence of what has been achieved." Working name "Revivo Impact" (tagline idea "Deliver sport. Demonstrate impact."). Portfolio: Revivo Pathway (talent ID, prototype done), Revivo Impact (next), Revivo Register (registration, eligibility & accreditation; runner-up). Says: don't rebuild Pathway, don't change the website immediately, no production commitment yet; products eventually work together without buying everything.
2. Evidence of the problem: Sport England evaluation framework (attendance, participant info, data responsibility); UK gov evaluation of Sport Survival Package (complaints about monitoring forms, difficulty producing financial info); 2026 academic study: S4D orgs lack funding/expertise for evaluation; Sport England evaluation support for 125+ partners; India CSR disclosures and impact assessments. Validates the problem, not willingness to pay. Root problem = fragmentation: paper registers, WhatsApp coach updates, Excel targets, accounting software, photo folders, Word/PowerPoint reports → manual consolidation → late reports. Value is EARLY visibility (know after the 2nd missed session, not at quarter end).
3. Competitors: Upshot (UK, from Football Foundation; monitoring, attendance, participants, outcomes, funder reporting; turnover-based pricing) and Views by Substance (UK; mobile attendance, participants, surveys, outcomes, case studies; Liverpool FC Foundation, Greenhouse Sports) are DIRECT competitors. Also JustGo (membership/registration, 100+ NGBs), Rosterfy (volunteers/events), SponsorCX (sponsorship). So mobile attendance, automated reports, dashboards, outcomes, photo evidence, AI summaries are NOT differentiation alone. Advantage must come from simpler implementation, better operational workflows, financial accountability, regional pricing, integration with athlete development.
4. Scorecard (weights: severity 20, frequency 15, buyer/budget 20, accessibility 20, competitive opportunity 15, fit 10): Impact 86, Register 80, Event Operations 78, Sponsorship 71, Facility 71, Safeguarding 70 (CPOMS, Clue, Globocol exist; high legal risk — not the 2nd product), AI Coaching 69.
5. Revivo advantages: sports department operations, Khelo Keralam, Pathway, working-group proposals, Keralam Sports Night (event), Dream Sports Foundation discussions (not customers). Prioritise accessible first customers.
6. Revivo Impact: answers for a funder (e.g. ₹1 crore): where is the money going, are programmes happening, how many genuinely benefit, which locations need support, what evidence supports achievements, should funding continue. Customers: primary = sports foundations, sport-for-development orgs, corporate CSR programme managers; secondary = federation development departments, pro club foundations, government sports departments; later = individual schools/academies. Target multi-site, multi-coach, recurring funded programmes.
7. Six modules (ONE connected workflow, not six dashboards): Programme Planning; Field Delivery (mobile sessions, attendance); Programme Monitoring (targets, missed sessions, gaps, corrective actions); Funding & Expenditure (budgets, expenses, documentation, reconcile approved spend); Evidence & Outcomes (surveys, indicators, consented stories, verification); Reporting & Accountability (reviewed reports linking activity, spend, evidence, results).
8. Signature: "Commitment-to-Evidence Chain": commitment (deliver 120 sessions, 6 centres, quarterly) → actual delivery (108/120, 104 verified, 4 awaiting) → expenditure (₹2.16L of ₹2.40L, 96% documented) → exceptions (12 not delivered: 8 weather, 4 coach availability; recovery plan approved) → reviewed funder statement. Funder can click a number to see where it came from (permissions apply). "Delivery records demonstrate activities; they do not independently establish social impact."
9. Programme manager home answers: what is happening, what is going wrong, what needs my attention, are we on track. Illustrative dashboard: "Play Every Day", Horizon Sports Foundation, 6 sites (Riverside School 20/20 on track, Hillview Academy 18/20 needs attention, Eastfield Centre 20/20, Greenpark School 14/20 behind, Westbrook Centre 17/20, Northgate School 19/20), 460 participants, 108/120 sessions, ₹2.16L/₹2.40L, 12 missed; recommended action "Review Greenpark's delivery schedule".
10. AI: deterministic for counts and budget utilisation; statistics for trends; rules for missing records; rules + probabilistic matching + review for duplicates; AI-assisted for summarising coach notes, translating, drafting funder reports grounded in verified data, explaining trends; humans approve expenditure and verify outcomes; causal claims need proper evaluation methodology. Signature AI: "Why is Greenpark behind and what should I discuss with the coordinator?" → retrieves recorded reasons, summarises, lists open corrective actions, drafts questions; every statement links to source; never invents an explanation. (Document mentions tagline "AI built for the pulse of sport" — NOTE: this is a RETIRED tagline on the website.)
11. Prototype: Pathway's quality as benchmark but distinct experience. Fictional world: Horizon Sports Foundation, "Play Every Day", 12 months, 6 schools/community centres, 460 participants, 12 coaches, quarterly target 120 sessions, ₹12 lakh/year (illustrative India deployment; software supports other currencies/terms), demo period Q2. Roles: funder representative, programme director, site coordinator, coach, finance officer, evaluation specialist, report approver. Journey: 1 establish programme (budget, expected results) 2 prepare sites (funding, coaches, schedules, targets) 3 coach delivers session on mobile (attendance + short update) 4 Greenpark falls behind → notification 5 site coordinator explains, submits revised schedule 6 finance officer records equipment purchase with document, requests approval 7 evaluation specialist reviews surveys, attendance trends, evidence limitations 8 AI drafts funder report from verified records 9 official reviews, corrects, approves 10 funder decides to continue/adjust. ~20 screens: portfolio, programme home; setup, commitments, site allocations; coach home, session register, participant profile; site comparison, exceptions, corrective actions; budget, expenditure entry, financial review; indicators, surveys, evidence library; report builder, review & approval, funder portal. Guided + hands-on modes like Pathway. EXCLUDE: accounting system, full grants management, fundraising CRM, athlete performance platform, independent impact evaluation service.
12. Commercial: annual organisational subscription (not per child). India price hypotheses: small ₹0.6–1.2L, multi-site ₹1.5–4L, large funder ₹5–15L, enterprise/government custom. Foundation-funded pilot first, then licence; government later.
13. Revivo Register (runner-up): verified participation and sporting eligibility across organisations and competitions (registration, guardian consent, documents, duplicate detection, age-category validation, affiliation, eligibility exceptions, appeals, competition entry, accreditation, QR check-in). Market mature (JustGo, ManageSports).
14. Long-term: products (what customers buy) vs capabilities (what they do). Pathway = talent ID/assessment/development; Impact = programme management + funding & impact reporting; Register = registration/eligibility/accreditation; Flow = events/volunteers/operations; Training = coaching/training/performance; Partner = sponsorship/activation/renewal. Dashboards, reporting, AI, comms, permissions, audit = shared capabilities. Did not review the live website; don't change website based only on this.
15. Risks: spreadsheets seen as sufficient (commercial); adding work for coaches (product); offline sync, multi-org permissions, security (technical); auto impact claims presented as verified (reputational).
16. Validate: Dream Sports Foundation, Pro Sport Development (Mizoram badminton evaluation), other foundations, CSR implementation partners, club foundations, federation development departments. 10 discovery questions (last report, how coaches record, number of systems, last problem found too late, hours per reporting cycle, linking spend to activity, who checks accuracy, software considered, who has budget, commit to pilot?). Go/no-go: ≥10 interviewed, ≥6 confirm recurring problems, ≥3 report templates reviewed, ≥2 competitor demos reviewed, ≥2 willing to test, ≥1 willing to discuss paid pilot.
17. Roadmap: 1 freeze & review Pathway; 2 Impact discovery (competitor teardown of Upshot and Views first); 3 Impact interactive prototype; 4 demos of Pathway + Impact to pick pilot; 5 production for a customer, or Register/Flow next. Don't build six polished prototypes before approaching customers.
18. Decision: next prototype Revivo Impact; category Sports Programme Delivery & Impact; segment multi-site foundations and funded programme operators; core problem: disconnect between funded commitments, delivery, expenditure and evidence; signature Commitment-to-Evidence Chain; AI = evidence-grounded operational & reporting assistant; annual subscription; runner-up Register; website changes deferred until portfolio review; no Pathway redesign; production conditional on validation.
