# Revivo Impact

**Sports funding & sponsorship platform — a Revivo Sportech prototype on fictional data (not yet built).**

Revivo Impact helps a foundation, company CSR team or federation run a sports scholarship or grant round from one
shareable link, choose recipients fairly, and then follow every award: money committed, money released, money spent and
checked, and the sporting progress it supports. Athletes, guardians, coaches, clubs and programme operators use it free
to find funding and apply from one reusable profile. Funding programmes (for example community sport delivered across
several sites) are followed the same way. Revivo Impact records commitments and payments; it never moves money.

Delivery and spending records show what was delivered and spent; they do not on their own prove social impact.
